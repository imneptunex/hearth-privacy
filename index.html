Hearth Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Hearth ("the app") is a virtual and mixed reality reading app for Meta Quest, made by Merve Yuvaci, an individual developer based in Turkey ("I", "me"). This policy explains what data the app uses, why, and how you can delete it.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Merve Yuvaci (Turkey) is the data controller. Contact: [CONTACT EMAIL]

2. Data that stays on your headset

  • Your reading progress, bookmarks, favorites, settings, comfort options and chosen environments.
  • Your written notes and drawings in books.
  • PDF and TXT files you import, and the names, authors and covers you give them. They are stored only on your device and are never uploaded.

3. Data used for the social features

When you open the Social tab, the app signs you in with your Meta account and stores the following on a server so that leaderboards, friends and shared underlines can work:

  • Meta user ID (to recognize your account). It is verified with Meta and is not shown to other readers.
  • Display name and profile photo link from your Meta Horizon profile. Your display name is used as your default reader name, and your photo is shown next to it. You can change your reader name in the app at any time (once every 14 days).
  • Reading statistics: minutes and pages read per day, your daily page goal, and for each book its title and how far you have read.
  • Friends: friend requests, your friends list and the readers you have blocked.
  • Public underlines: if you use the public pen, the position of the underlined sentence in the book (a number range, not the text). Other readers see only how many readers underlined a sentence, never who. No free drawings and no written notes are ever shared.
  • Your privacy settings and the time you were last active.

4. How the data is used

Only to run the app's features: leaderboards, friend lists, showing your reading to friends, your daily goal, and showing public underlines. I do not sell your data, do not use it for advertising and do not share it with advertisers.

5. Who can see what

  • Leaderboard: your reader name, photo, minutes and pages. You can switch "Show me on the public leaderboard" off.
  • Friends: your books, progress and today's reading time. You can switch "Friends can see what I read" off.
  • Public underlines: anonymous counts. You can hide other readers' underlines with a switch.
  • You can remove a friend or block any reader at any time.

6. Service providers

Account and social data is stored and processed on my behalf by Supabase (cloud database and server functions). Supabase processes data only to provide this hosting service. Meta also receives data as described in Meta's own privacy policy. Servers may be located outside Turkey.

7. How long data is kept, and deleting it

Social data is kept until you delete it. In the app, open Social, then "Me & privacy", then "Delete my social account" to remove your profile, statistics, friends, blocks and underlines from the server. You can also email me at the address above to ask for deletion. Uninstalling the app removes the data stored on your headset.

8. Your rights

Under Turkish data protection law (KVKK) and, where applicable, other laws such as the GDPR, you can ask what data I hold about you, ask for corrections, ask for deletion, and object to processing. Email me at the address above.

9. Requests from public authorities

If a public authority asks for user data, I review the legality of the request, share only the minimum information required and keep a record of the request and my response. I have not provided user data to public authorities.

10. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect data from them. Use of Meta Quest is governed by Meta's age rules.

11. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and access to the database is restricted to the app's server functions. No system is perfectly secure, but I take reasonable care to protect your data.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated and, for material changes, the app will tell you.
